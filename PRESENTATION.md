## The "Legal Black Box" 📦
*   **Density:** Average contracts are 20+ pages of "Legalese."
*   **Cost:** Legal review costs $300-$1000+ per hour.
*   **Risk:** 40% of small businesses face legal disputes due to poorly understood terms.

_**Speaker Note:** Start by highlighting the anxiety people feel when they see "Click here to agree" or a 50-page employment contract. It's a black box that costs too much to open._

## The Solution: Fortress AI 🛡️
Fortress AI acts as a **High-Fidelity Bridge** between complex legal jargon and actionable insights.

*   **Instant:** Analysis in seconds.
*   **Contextual:** Advice tailored to *your* specific role.
*   **Local-First:** Privacy-focused legal processing.

---

## How it Works: The Audit Pipeline ⚙️
1.  **Parsing:** Structural decomposition of PDFs.
2.  **Extraction:** Identifying key clauses (Liability, Term, IP).
3.  **Risk Scoring:** AI-driven severity assessment.
4.  **Synthesis:** Generating a professional verdict & report.

---

## The "Verdict" System ⚖️
We simplify decision-making into four clear paths:

*   🟢 **SIGN:** Standard, low-risk terms.
*   🟡 **NEGOTIATE:** Specific language needs adjustment.
*   🟠 **SEEK COUNSEL:** High complexity detected.
*   🔴 **REJECT:** Predatory or extreme liability.
